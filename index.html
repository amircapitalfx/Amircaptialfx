How to set up Telegram
Set up Telegram: 1) In Telegram open @BotFather, send /newbot and copy the token. 2) Open your new bot, press Start and send it any message. 3) Paste the token here, tap Find my chat ID, then Send Telegram test, then switch Telegram on. The token stays in this browser only. Turn Telegram on for one device only, or you will get every message twice. Alerts run while this site is open. A closed tab, or a phone that has put the browser to sleep, will not send them.

Why the demo shows losses, and what live needs

The demo feed is random prices, and a trend-pullback system has no edge on random prices. Seven trades is also too few to judge anything. To take this live:
  • Load real candles for each market from your broker, at least 1,000 rows.
  • Check 100+ signals per market. Net R should be positive across different months, not just one good run.
  • With 1:2 to 1:3, you break even at 25% to 33% wins before costs. Spread and slippage push that up, so you want 35%+ in the test.
  • Forward test on a demo account for 4 to 8 weeks and log every trade here.
  • Go live at 0.25% to 0.5% risk until 50 live trades match the test.
  • Skip high-impact news. Trend systems suit GBPJPY, XAUUSD and NAS100 better than ranging pairs like EURGBP.
  • Automatic live prices need a data provider and a small server, because this page cannot fetch prices itself.
